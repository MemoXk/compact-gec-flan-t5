Nowadays , public transport has become a major topic .
The idea of leaving public transportation aside is widely supported in developed countries , but this idea is rejected in developing countries , like the one I live in .
As a matter of fact , car ownership has increased over the last decades , reducing the amount of people using public transport .
The possibility of traveling in their own cars is much more convenient than going by a bus full of people , or even worse , the idea of wasting time in a long queue .
Nevertheless , you have another side to this reality .
There are people who can not afford to have their own cars or may be they can afford to travel by any other means of transportation than those provided by the local government .
There are countries where gas is so expensive that people choose to pay the bus fare rather than pay for a full tank .
Similarly , people in countries with less income turn to these services because they are a way to reduce their overall expenses .
To conclude , the future of public transport will depend on how much people use it .
There will be countries in which there will be no need for it , and in others where they will need it as long as their economies need it .
People need easy , fast , and cheap solutions .
In general , using a car is easier than taking a train or a bus .
It 's just near your house or your office .
You do n't have to go to a station by walking , carrying weights , or suffering because of the rain or the snow .
But if you are sleepy , tired , bored , or simply you do n't like driving , you can find it easier to take a public transport .
And also more enjoyable : you can relax listening to the music and looking around , or reading newspapers and books .
Is the car faster than public transport ?
It depends .
The traffic affects both cars and buses at the same time .
Sometimes cars are faster than buses , sometimes buses are faster .
Speed trains are always faster than cars .
Speaking about money ? Travelling by car seems cheaper than public transport .
But , if we consider all the expenses ( buying the car , paying the insurance , etc . ) , maybe cars are not so cheap .
In fact , in every aspect , there are pros and cons .
What about the environment and the quality of life in the cities ?
Public transportation is absolutely better .
So , even though traveling by car may be more convenient personally , public transport should be preferred by good citizens and strongly supported by governments .

Dickens and the Brontë sisters use the same style : realistic style .
They are influenced by their surroundings : Dickens lived in the city of London , while the Brontë sisters lived in the natural landscape of Yorkshire .
Dickens and Charlotte Bronte often use the first - person narrator or the point of view of the protagonist of the story .
Their realism differs : in fact , Dickens ' style is that of sentimental realism , in which he mixes pathos and comedy , such as characters who tend to be caricatures of the vices and virtues he wishes to portray ; whereas the Brontë sisters use a realistic style tinged with Romantic and Gothic elements , such as Byronic male heroes and female protagonists that are psychologically complex .

In many of the minority works of modern literature , there can be some emphasis on rebellion against oppressive forces .
Although such a display of strength by these work 's characters is sometimes viewed as a violent and therefore negative contribution to the otherwise insightful thought of the author , it is this conflict upon which many stories of ethnic American literature are based .
However , such a rebellion can not be seen clearly in each minority work , and , therefore , the products of ethnic American literature can not be categorized solely as the result of years of oppression .
Rather , this ever - changing and ever - challenging aspect of minority literature creates an especially important necessity : each work must be considered individually , as both a product of years of struggle and a work inherently distinct from any other .
In the minority works , the conflict and rebellion presented accurately reflects society as a whole ; although the mother 's in " The Joy Luck Club " rebel against such forces as arranged marriages , Tita in " Like Water for Chocolate " fights to overcome old family traditions , and the Indian tribes in " Love Medicine " struggle to secure their inherent rights from the US government , the characters ' conflicts reflect similar conflicts of other cultures .
Rebellion is seen not as a distinct force in minority cultures , but rather as a binding force between all cultures , for Celie , too , resists her arranged marriage in " The Color Purple , " and the murderous loves in " Jazz " reflect Tita and Pedro 's destructive passion in " Like Water for Chocolate . "
Rebellion and conflict are emphasized in ethnic American literature , but they are portrayed no more strongly in minority works than in other pieces of literature .
Emphasis changes with each word , and although figures of authority are particularly oppressive in works such as Like Water for Chocolate and The Color Purple , other minority works , including Love Medicine and Jazz , do not reflect the clearly defined authoritarian figures nor the obvious rebellion of the characters ' responsive actions , which the previously mentioned works show .
This again implies that these ethnic American pieces of literature can not be categorized solely as rebellious responses to oppression , but as individual reflections of personal and cultural experiences .
How does Voltaire address the issue of philosophical optimism in ' Candide ' ?
Philosophical optimism - l'optimisme - is the philosophy that everything and every occurrence is for some good .
Voltaire sets out in Candide to criticize this philosophy .
In Candide , the representative of this philosophy is Pangloss , the philosopher employed at Thunder - der - Troncks .
The entire book is a critique of optimism .
For example , when Candide is forced to join the Bulgarian army , he is beaten almost to death ; however , Candide does not concern himself because he knows it is for his own good — indeed , later in the book , he uses his training to become a soldier .
Although this seems to be good , it is not because of his training that he kills two men : a Jew and an Archbishop with no hesitation , and then proceeds to kill his sweetheart 's brother .
Voltaire shows his dislike of optimism when Pangloss , Candide , and James are caught in a bad storm at sea . In this storm , James is drowned , leaving only Candide , Pangloss , and a sailor as survivors .
The storm was caused by an earthquake that destroyed the city to which Candide was sailing .
On arrival , they see starvation and death .
Candide is obviously horrified , especially when the sailor begins pillaging the city .
Candide looks to Pangloss for an answer ; however , Pangloss can only tell him that some good will come of it .
Candide , although remaining true to the ideals of optimism , always appears to be skeptical of it .
This is not any truer than when he arrives in the New World , South America , and has to leave his beloved Cunégonde with the Commissioner of the province .
Candide can not see what possible good can come of this , especially since the reason he was in America was due to him killing the Jew and Archbishop because they were Cunégonde 's lovers .
Throughout the book , we begin to see how Candide is gradually led away from optimism , or perhaps more importantly , how optimism is shown to be the philosophy of despair .
The constant belief that good can come from bad is not actually inspiring ; instead , it leads to a constant flow of disasters .
Candide 's life , in the book , appears to show this .
He is thrown out of Thunder - Den - Tronk because he embraces Cunégonde ; he is duped into the army ; he is caught in an earthquake ; he is almost killed as part of an auto - da - fé ; he kills three churchmen ; he is almost eaten ; he finds Eldorado but leaves . The list continues .
Candide 's life is governed by his belief that good will prevail .
Eventually , of course , he does marry Cunégonde , but she is not the beautiful young woman that he once loved .
She has grown ugly and worsened by the day .
This illustrates emphatically the folly of optimism .
Candide has traveled the world in search of Cunégonde , with the hope that he will marry her despite all the trouble he has gone through ; however , in the end , he does get Cunégonde , but at the cost of her looks and personality .
Voltaire , in Candide , shows how the philosophy of optimism is one of despair , not hope .

The European Community is a unique grouping of twelve sovereign European member states who are committed to the development of closer economic and political cooperation .
The community aims to break down the economic and political barriers that have traditionally divided Europe .
In particular , the community seeks to facilitate greater movement within its borders ; movement not only of goods but of services , capital , and people .
However , the issue of sovereignty is certainly a much - debated one ; to what extent should a member hand over its sovereignty to the Community , if at all ?
Let us look at the founding of the European Community , before we discuss the issue of sovereignty in more detail .
There were several attempts at peaceful unification after the First World War , but only after Europe had again been devastated by war did the futility of national rivalries truly become apparent .
The political and economic exhaustion of the European states called for a new start and a more radical approach to the reordering of Europe .
This fresh start came about because of three major factors .
Firstly , Europe 's realization of her own weakness occurred after losing her longtime position as the center of the world stage to the United States of America and the Soviet Union .
These superpowers were economically , militarily , and politically stronger than the divided individual European states .
Secondly , the conviction that military conflict should be avoided in the future ; after all , the two great wars had both begun as European ' civil wars ' . Thirdly , the common desire for a better , freer world in which international relations would be conducted in an orderly manner .
One of the first major steps towards European integration was the creation of the European Economic Community , in 1958 .
Its membership has grown from the ten original founding countries to twenty - three .
One of the most innovative features of the European Community compared with other international bodies is that the member states have ceded to it a part of their national sovereignty and endowed it with its own sovereign powers , which it can use to adopt acts , having the force of national law .
This novel approach to pooling national sovereignty is called " integration " .
The 1970 's saw progress in integration , with the introduction of community policy instruments , which enabled greater scope for national policies .
All of the member states agreed in principle to the idea of a single market by 1992 , however , each wished to safeguard its own interests .

The situation is seen as a threat to the countries in question , who do not wish to surrender their sovereignty for the sake of European unity .

The discovery of penicillin as an antibiotic was one of the greatest advances in medical chemistry .
As a natural antibiotic , penicillin was discovered to have many uses in treating and preventing the spread of infections .
This " wonder - drug " also led to the discovery of a variety of other naturally - occurring antibiotics that could also be cultured " in vitro " in a laboratory .
This sudden burst of useful compounds not only improved the chances of a patient 's survival in a hospital but also caused a great need for medical chemists to study and classify each new drug as it was discovered .
As discoveries of new antibiotics decreased , chemists began to wonder if certain variations of these drugs could be synthesized and also used as medicines .
It was attempted to increase the production of the naturally - occurring antibiotics through synthesis .
Both of these new attempts caused a need for more research , more companies , and more medical chemists , and the industry increased tremendously .
It was discovered that the older drugs could be synthesized efficiently , yet remained biologically active .
New drugs were also synthesized ; some were found to be medically useful , while others were not .
A variety of new drugs constantly appeared on the market and changed the way people thought about taking medicine .
It was now much easier to obtain antibiotics for simple illnesses , such as the common cold .
Huge enterprises , such as Upjohn , were formed and grew , and universities had research teams devoted to producing efficient synthesis routes for the production of all kinds of antibiotics .
The discovery of one compound led to the improvement of millions of peoples ' health , to the discovery of more and more antibiotics , and to a booming industry comprised of research teams and large companies .
The synthesis of antibiotics and research of their uses still continues today . As thousands of universities and companies around the world continue the work of a small bacterium -- the producer of penicillin-- , new antibiotics are being discovered .

When Voltaire wrote Candide , a literary style known as neoclassicism was at its peak .
There was a group of scholars in France , Académie Française , that set guidelines for French literature .
According to Académie Française , all literature of the Neoclassical period must follow the rules of propriety , which regulate authors to avoid certain topics , including sex , violence , church , and state issues .
Another standard upheld by the Academy is the idea of verisimilitude , making literature as realistic as possible by keeping the time , setting , and plot true to life .
Voltaire , as a neoclassical writer , knew all these regulations and followed them in his literature .
However , when writing Candide , Voltaire defied the Academy 's rules .
Candide is a parody of the principles of Neoclassicism .
Neoclassicists liked for literature to have good manners : they did not want to step on anyone 's toes by mentioning sex , violence , church , or state issues .
In Candide , Voltaire did not seem to mind stepping on toes – in fact , he did not mind smashing feet !
Voltaire went beyond his comfort level when he mentioned sex .
He told of the two girls from Orellon who were lovers of monkeys , and of the Baron who bathed with the Musselmans and was punished for his homosexual acts .
Sex turned into violence when Cunegonde was being raped by a barber , and she witnessed his murder while he was on top of her .
The wise old woman underwent the removal of her buttocks by pirates , as well as experiencing rape .
After the Baron is caught bathing with the Muslim , he receives 100 lashes for his sin .
Candide stabs the Baron , a Jesuit priest , after the Baron forbids Candide 's marriage to Cunegonde .
Ironically , although Voltaire mentions many acts of violence , each act is treated lightly : Cunegonde and the old woman both tell their tales of being defiled like an entertaining story in their conversations about their travels ; the Baron also continues his tale with style after telling of his beating ; and after Candide " kills " the Baron , he leaves to search for Cunegonde with no second thought until he reencounters the Baron later in his travels .
The light - hearted approach in which these acts of sex and violence are portrayed shows the parody style in which Voltaire wrote Candide .
Voltaire does not choose to stay away from church and state issues .
He attracts the attention of the Church in the old woman 's father being the Pope .
When Candide visits the land of Eldorado , Voltaire shows the people 's perfect relationship to God : they only praise God because there is nothing they want from God .
Voltaire uses this perfect relationship to God in an unrealistic situation to foil society 's imperfect relationship with God in the real world .
Voltaire makes fun of aristocracy in his description of the Baron , who comes from 71 generations of nobility but still lacks gentile spirit .
Candide and his companions dine with six deposed kings who represent the over - importance society places on hierarchy .
In his portrayal of the utopian society of Eldorado , Voltaire highlights the imperfections of our society .

Our country has been called a " melting - pot " implying that we all come from different backgrounds but have joined or melted together to produce homogeneous Americans .
But people 's heritage , as we are realizing , is important to their sense of self and community .
Ethnic American cultures have been revitalizing and trying to re - establish roots that were once cut off to become a model U. S. citizen .
The differences they are rediscovering add more color , flavor , and excitement to that brewing " pot " of our people .
However , some individuals and even groups of Americans are reacting negatively to this ethnic explosion ( seen most clearly through the arts ) .
They do n't want to accept these differences .
They choose to remain indifferent to the plight of their fellow Americans .
As Ellie Wiesel , author of such books as Night , says about indifference : it is the opposite of everything good .
The opposite of love , beauty , intelligence , light , joy , life , and growth are not their familiar antonyms but indifference .
Ethnic American literature tries to fight this indifference by vividly and uniquely expressing certain aspects of different American cultures .
The range of ethnic American literature is without bounds .
Each group has its own peculiarities and attributes that make its writing unique .
In many cases , the words used in ethnic American literature reflect a knowledge of a different " mother tongue " .
For example , Native American poetry is more expressive and visual , partly because their language is deeply rooted in the observation of the natural environment .
Chinese American literature is philosophical or literal because their native language is so .
African American literature is often melodic and rhythmic , mirroring their similar achievements in jazz and blues music .
All these examples ( and more ) give us some insight into other cultures that we would otherwise never know .
The stories told in this genre of literature , however , are likely the most important aspect of ethnic American literature .
It is through the experiences of the characters that we learn about the different cultures in the United States .
When we learn about the trials and hardships that they went through , we can sympathize with their emotions and try to accept that diversity .
The stories of Black American slaves and of Holocaust victims are necessary to avoid indifference .
Without learning about the past ( and present ) horrors , there is no way to ensure future humanity .
Ethnic American literature benefits U. S. citizens in many ways .
It opens our eyes to the realities of the suffering of others .
It gives us a fresh and new perspective on what 's around us .
It helps us to understand our personal relationships more clearly .
Most importantly , it gives us more common ground on which to live , grow , and prosper .
Hopefully , we will someday fulfill Elie Weisel 's goal and end indifference ; this literature is a step in the right direction toward acceptance in the diversity of our country .

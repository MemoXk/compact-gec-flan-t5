The idea of leaving public transportation aside is widely supported in developed countries , but this idea is rejected in developing countries , such as the one I live in .

The possibility of traveling in their own cars is much more convenient than going by bus with many people or worse , the idea of spending time in a long queue .
Nevertheless , you have another side to this reality .
There are people who can not afford to have their own cars , or perhaps they can afford to travel by other means of transportation than those provided by the local government .
There are countries where gas is so expensive that people choose to pay the bus fare rather than fill up their tanks .
Similarly , people in countries with less income turn to these services because it is a way to reduce their overall expenses .
To conclude , the future of public transport will depend on how much people use it .
There will be countries where there will be no need for it , and in others where they will need it as long as their economies need it .
People need easy , fast , and cheap solutions .
In general , using a car is easier than taking a train or a bus .

You do n't have to go to a station by walking , carrying weights , or suffering because of the rain or the snow .

Is the car faster than public transportation ?
It depends .
The traffic can affect cars and buses at the same level .
Sometimes cars are faster than buses , and sometimes buses are faster .
Speed trains are always faster than cars .
Speaking about money ? Travelling by car seems cheaper than public transport .

In many of the minority works of modern literature , there can be some emphasis on rebellion against oppressive forces .
Although such a show of strength by these work 's characters is sometimes viewed as a violent and therefore negative contribution to the otherwise insightful thoughts of the author , it is this conflict upon which many stories of ethnic American literature are based .
However , such a rebellion can not be seen clearly in each minority work , and , therefore , the products of ethnic American literature can not be categorized solely as the result of years of oppression .
Rather , this ever - changing and ever - challenging aspect of minority literature creates an especially important necessity : each work should be considered individually , as both a product of years of struggle and a work inherently distinct from any other .
In the minority works , the conflict and rebellion presented accurately reflects society as a whole , and although the mother 's in " The Joy Luck Club " rebel against such forces as arranged marriages , Tita in " Like Water for Chocolate " fights to overcome old family traditions , and the Indian tribes in " Love Medicine " struggle to secure their inherent rights from the US government , the characters ' conflicts reflect similar conflicts of other cultures .
Rebellion is viewed not as a separate force in minority cultures , but rather as a unifying force among all cultures . For Celie , who resists her arranged marriage in " The Color Purple , " and in " Like Water for Chocolate , " where Tita and Pedro 's destructive passions are reflected , the rebellious spirit also manifests itself .
Rebellion and conflict are emphasized in ethnic American literature , but they are portrayed no more strongly in minority works than in other pieces of literature .
Emphasis changes with each work , and although figures of authority are particularly oppressive in works like Like Water for Chocolate and The Color Purple , other minority works , including Love Medicine and Jazz , do not reflect the clearly defined authoritarian figures or the obvious rebellions of the characters ' responsive actions , which the previously mentioned works show .
This again implies that ethnic American pieces of literature can not be categorized solely as rebellious responses to oppression ; they are also individual reflections of personal and cultural experiences .

Voltaire sets out in Candide to criticize this philosophy .

The European Community is a unique grouping of twelve sovereign European member states who are committed to the development of closer economic and political cooperation .
The community aims to break down the economic and political barriers that have traditionally divided Europe .
In particular , the community seeks to facilitate greater movement within its borders ; movement not only of goods but of services , capital , and people .
However , the issue of sovereignty is certainly a much - debated one ; to what extent should a member hand over its sovereignty to the Community , if at all ?
Let 's look at the founding of the European Community , before we discuss the issue of sovereignty in more detail .
There were several attempts at peaceful unification after the First World War , but only after Europe had again been devastated by war did the futility of national rivalries become truly apparent .
The political and economic exhaustion of the European states called for a fresh start and a more radical approach to the re - ordering of Europe .
This fresh start came about due to three main factors .
Firstly , Europe 's realization of her own weakness occurred after losing her age - old position at the center of the world stage to the United States of America and the Soviet Union .
These superpowers were economically , militarily , and politically stronger than the divided individual European states .
Secondly , the conviction that military conflict should be avoided in the future was shared ; after all , the two great wars had both begun as European " civil wars " . Thirdly , there was a common desire for a better , freer world where international relations would be conducted in an orderly manner .

Its membership has grown from the ten original founding countries to twenty - three .
One of the most innovative features of the European Community compared with other international bodies is that the member states have ceded some of their national sovereignty and endowed it with its own sovereign powers , which it can use to adopt acts , having the force of national law .
This novel approach to pooling national sovereignty is called " integration " .

This sudden burst of useful compounds not only improved the chances of a patient 's survival in a hospital but also caused a great need for medical chemists to study and classify each new drug as it was discovered .
As discoveries of new antibiotics decreased , chemists began to wonder if certain variations of these drugs could be synthesized and also used as medicines .
It was attempted to increase the production of the naturally - occurring antibiotics through synthesis .
Both of these new attempts caused a need for more research , more companies , and more medical chemists , and the industry increased tremendously .
It was discovered that older drugs could be synthesized efficiently while remaining biologically active .
New drugs were also synthesized ; some were found to be medically useful , while others were not .
A variety of new drugs continually appeared on the market , changing how people thought about taking medicine .
It was now much easier to obtain antibiotics for simple illnesses , such as the common cold .
Large companies like Upjohn were established and grew , and universities had research teams focused on developing efficient synthesis routes for producing various antibiotics .
The discovery of one compound led to the increased health of millions of people ; to the discovery of more and more antibiotics ; and to a booming industry comprised of research teams and large companies .
The synthesis of antibiotics and research of their uses still continues today . Thousands of universities and companies worldwide continue the work of a small bacterium — the producer of penicillin .

When Voltaire wrote Candide , a literary style known as neoclassicism was at its peak .
There was a group of scholars in France , the Académie Française , that set guidelines for French literature .
According to the Académie Française , all literature from the Neoclassical period must adhere to the rules of propriety that regulate authors should avoid certain topics , including sex , violence , church , and state issues .
Another standard upheld by the Academy is the idea of verisimilitude , making literature as realistic as possible by keeping the time , setting , and plot true to life .
Voltaire , as a neoclassical writer , knew all these regulations and followed them in his literature .
However , when writing Candide , Voltaire defied the Academy 's rules .
Candide is a parody of the neoclassical guidelines .
Neoclassicists liked for literature to have good manners : they did not want to step on anyone 's toes by mentioning sex , violence , church , or state issues .
In Candide , Voltaire did n't seem to mind stepping on toes – in fact , he did n't mind smashing feet !
Voltaire went beyond his comfort level when he mentioned sex .
He told of the two girls from Orellin who were lovers of monkeys , and of the Baron who bathed with the Musselmans and was punished for his homosexual acts .
Sex turned into violence when Cunegonde was being raped by a barber , and she watched him murdered while he was on top of her .
The wise old woman underwent the removal of her buttocks by pirates , and she experienced rape .
After the baron is caught bathing with the musulman , he receives 100 lashes for his sin .
Candide stabs the Baron , a Jesuit priest , after the Baron forbids Candide 's marriage to Cunegonde .
Ironically , although Voltaire mentions many acts of violence , each act is treated lightly : Cunegonde and the old woman both tell their tales of being defiled like an entertaining story in their conversations about their travels ; the Baron also continues his tale with style after telling of his beating ; and after Candide " kills " the Baron , he leaves to search for Cunegonde with no second thought , until he reencounters the Baron later in his travels .
The light - hearted attitude in which these acts of sex and violence are written shows the parody form in which Voltaire wrote Candide .
Voltaire does not choose to avoid church and state issues .
He attracts the hypocrisy of the Church in the old woman 's father being the Pope .
When Candide visits the land of Eldorado , Voltaire shows the people 's perfect relationship to God : they only praise God because there is nothing they want from God .
Voltaire uses this perfect relationship to God in an unrealistic situation to foil society 's imperfect relationship with God in the real world .
Voltaire makes fun of aristocracy in his description of the Baron , who comes from 71 generations of nobility but still lacks gentilé .
Candide and his companions dine with six deposed kings who represent the overemphasis society places on hierarchy .
In his portrayal of the utopian society of Eldorado , Voltaire highlights the imperfections of our society .

Our country has been called a " melting - pot , " implying that we all come from different backgrounds and have joined or melted together to produce homogeneous Americans .
But a person 's heritage , as we are realizing , is important to their sense of self and community .
Ethnic American cultures have been revitalizing and attempting to re - establish roots that were once cut off to become a model US citizen .
The differences they are rediscovering add more color , flavor , and excitement to that brewing " pot " of our people .
However , some individuals and even groups of Americans are reacting negatively to this ethnic explosion ( seen most clearly through the arts ) .
They do n't want to accept these differences .
They choose to remain indifferent to the plight of their fellow Americans .
As Ellie Weisel , author of such books as " Night , " says about indifference : it is the opposite of everything good .
The opposite of love , beauty , intelligence , light , joy , life , and growth are not their familiar antonyms but indifference .
Ethnic American literature strives to combat this indifference by vividly and uniquely expressing various aspects of different American cultures .
The range of ethnic American literature is vast .
Each group has its own peculiarities and attributes that make its writing unique .
In many cases , the words used in ethnic American literature reflect a knowledge of a different " mother tongue " .
For example , Native American poetry is more expressive and visual , partly because their language is deeply rooted in observing the natural environment .
Chinese American literature is philosophical and literal because their native language is so complex .
African American literature is often melodic and rhythmic , mirroring their achievements in jazz and blues music .
All these examples , and more , give us some insight into other cultures that we would otherwise never know .
The stories told in this genre of literature are likely the most important aspect of ethnic American literature .
It is through the experiences of the characters that we learn about the various cultures in the United States .
When we learn of the trials and hardships that they went through , we can sympathize with their emotions and try to accept that diversity .
The stories of Black American slaves and Holocaust victims are necessary to avoid indifference .
Without learning about the past and present horrors , there is no way to ensure future humanity .
Ethnic American literature benefits US citizens in many ways .
It opens our eyes to the reality of other 's suffering .
It provides us with a fresh and new perspective on the world around us .
It helps us to understand our personal relationships even better .
Most of all , it gives us more common ground on which to live , grow , and prosper .
Hopefully , we will someday fulfill Elie Weisel 's goal and end indifference ; this literature is a step in the right direction toward acceptance in the diversity of our country .

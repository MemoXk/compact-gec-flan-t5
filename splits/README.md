# Splits

* `c4_split_index_clean.tsv.gz` / `c4_split_index_noisy.tsv.gz`: for every one of the 1,200,000 C4-200M pairs kept by the cleaned (resp. uncleaned) sampling, the shard number, the line number inside `C4_200M.tsv-0000x-of-00010` and the split (`train`, `val`, `test`). Regenerating the corpus with `notebooks/R2_A_cleaning_full_budget.ipynb` gives the same rows; the cleaned test split is verified by the SHA-256 stored in `configs/splits.json`.
* `c4_test_subset_5k.tsv`: the fixed 5,000-pair in-domain evaluation subset (`test_index` = row of the 25,000-pair cleaned test split; subset drawn with `numpy.random.default_rng(2026).permutation(25000)[:5000]`, sorted). C4-200M is by Stahlberg and Kumar (2021) and is based on C4 (Raffel et al., 2020); please cite both.
* `wi_sentence_essay.tsv`: for each of the 34,308 W&I+LOCNESS v2.1 training pairs (A+B+C M2 file order), its band and essay id in the corpus's JSON release (3,000 essays; every sentence aligns).
* `wi_holdout_essay_index.txt`: indices of the 1,623 pairs of the essay-level stage-2 holdout (5% of the essays of each band, seed 42; 150 essays). Training pairs whose source sentence also occurs in a held-out essay (371) are removed, leaving 32,314 training pairs.
* `c4_test_subset_uncontaminated.txt`: row numbers (0-based) of the 626 in-domain test pairs that occur in neither C4 training split; the filtering contrast is scored in domain on these rows only.
* `round2_wi_holdout_index_sentence_level.txt`: the superseded round-2 holdout (5% of unique source sentences); 1,704 of its 1,708 pairs came from essays that also supplied training pairs.
